## Durable queue recovery

Run the queue store, broker, transport and controller suites with real temporary directories and synthetic keys. Cover exact account/profile/conversation isolation, explicit profile resolution, active-profile changes, lock cycles, session reclaim, commit failure, lost and malformed acceptance, concurrent dispatch claims, and captured-scope deletion.

Use the shared client-isolation procedure before launching the native fixture. Queue synthetic text plus an attachment while a reply runs. Confirm enqueue completion precedes composer clearing. Stop the isolated app, remove the original attachment, restart it, and confirm the queue remains paused with retained bytes. Neither reply completion nor session-settle events may submit before review. Send next must submit one entry; remaining restored entries stay paused. Switch profiles, including same-ID sessions, and confirm no payload crosses scopes. An unavailable chat already known before activity recovery keeps its original remote ID and cannot become a new session. Cold restart does not enumerate conversations absent from the server list; their exact-scope records remain encrypted on disk.

Interrupt a submit after dispatch but before known acceptance. Restart and confirm the uncertain entry offers inspection and removal, with no send control or automatic duplicate. Exercise App Lock while a queue operation is pending; visible DTOs and leases must be invalidated, and unlock must restore review. Failed writes keep composer text and attachments. Delete a conversation during a profile change and confirm only the captured original scope is removed. Reject refresh credentials and confirm records stay encrypted while DTOs and leases disappear. Reverify the same owner and confirm paused recovery without a submit. Explicit sign-out after expiry must clear only the remembered owner scope.

For actual macOS keychain persistence, inject FlutterSecureStorage with a unique nonempty fixture accountName on both Mac and iOS options. The pinned plugin maps this to kSecAttrService and preserves it for deletion. Keep unlocked_this_device, synchronizable false and the production Debug data-protection setting. Disable Secure Enclave options. Assert macOS and the fixture service before any call. All auth, token and preference storage remains in memory. Never print key values; cleanup only the exact task service and verified task-owned queue directory. The native queueDirectory channel can establish backup exclusion and mode 0700 on macOS. iOS physical file-protection and device lifecycle checks remain separate.
