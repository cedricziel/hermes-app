Hermes Agent Companion is a client for your own Hermes Agent dashboard. Point it at your self-hosted server, sign in, and check on your agent from your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

This is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nous Research, and it needs a Hermes Agent server that you run yourself.

WHAT YOU GET

• Connect to any self-hosted Hermes Agent dashboard by its address
• Sign in with OpenID Connect or a username and password. Sign-in happens in your system browser, so your password manager works
• Session tokens are kept in the system keychain and refreshed automatically
• See your server's status and the account you are signed in with
• Works on a dashboard with sign-in turned off, such as a local development setup

PRIVACY

No account with us, no analytics, no tracking. The app only talks to the server address you enter.
