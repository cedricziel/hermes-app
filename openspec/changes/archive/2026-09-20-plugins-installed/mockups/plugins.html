Plugins screen – mockups

Proposal for a native Plugins screen in the Hermes app. Opened from a “Plugins” row in the chat sidebar footer, next to Profiles and Bots. Three tabs: Installed, Catalog, Providers. Illustrative, not pixel-exact; the real screen uses the app’s Material 3 theme.

Where these differ from change 1 (plugins-installed):
  • The “Update available” pill and the sidebar badge are not in part 1. The hub has no such fact; they come with plugins-catalog.
  • In part 1 the details show Update for any plugin the server marks updatable, and the answer is “Updated” or “Already up to date”.
  • Part 1 has no tab row: only the Installed list. The Catalog and Providers tabs (shown here) belong to changes 2 and 3.
  • For the kanban plugin the hide switch says it also removes the Kanban tab from the app.
