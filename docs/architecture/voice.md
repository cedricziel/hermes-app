# Voice

**Voice** (`lib/src/voice/`). Dictation follows assistant-ui's dictation element: the composer's microphone (shown when `GET /api/audio/voice-config` reports usable speech-to-text for the chat's profile; `stt disabled` or `no credentials` hide it) records 16 kHz mono PCM with `record` (`voice_recorder.dart`), and, after the Claude app, the field stays read-only with the words recognized so far at the cursor (`DictationDraft`, which gives the draft back on cancel or failure) while the controls row shows cancel, the waveform (`widgets/voice_waveform.dart`) with the engine's name, stop and send. Send while dictating stops, waits for the transcript (`DictationController.whenSettled`) and sends the draft, but nothing after a failed transcription; otherwise the transcript is never sent. `DictationController` streams the audio to the `/api/audio/transcribe-stream` socket when `stt.streaming` says the provider can transcribe live (`TranscribeStream`, opened by `hermesMixedSocketConnect` with the same ticket or token as `/api/ws`), and otherwise, or when that fails, uploads the recording as WAV through `HermesChatRepository.transcribe` with a longer timeout (`receiveTimeoutExtra`, applied by `RequestTimeoutInterceptor` on every client `AuthController` builds). A connector that throws (even synchronously) only fails the live stream, so the upload takes over; any other failure while starting ends the dictation as failed, once, and nothing is sent. Stopping the recorder is attempted on every path (a recorder that throws on stop is contained, even synchronously, and the microphone is not guaranteed closed then): a recorder whose `start` throws, or whose stream a superseded start cannot listen to, is stopped; a recorder that throws on stop ends `stop()` as failed (once, nothing sent) and `cancel()` as cancelled, never leaving the phase stuck or throwing. It holds an `stt-lease` while recording, drops the recording when the app leaves the foreground or the profile changes, and records breadcrumbs without text. On iOS and macOS dictation defaults to the device's own recognizer (`DictationSettings`; the Dictation setting in the account menu and Settings… switches to Hermes, and a language the device cannot recognize falls back to it): `packages/hermes_speech` (a local plugin, one Swift source for both) runs Apple's `SpeechAnalyzer` on the same PCM, and `OnDeviceSpeech` wraps its channels, one app-wide instance because the event channel has a single native listener. With it no `/api/audio/` route is called and no recording is kept; `ChatScreen` fetches a missing model by itself and shows the microphone once it is installed (each app has to request it once even when the system already has it); the waveform says which engine is listening. Conversation windows offer no dictation, so neither `record` nor `hermes_speech` is registered in their engine. The watch has no recognizer (watchOS has no Speech framework); its voice messages reach the phone as AAC (`transcribe`), and `WatchBridge.onDeviceTranscriber` runs `OnDeviceSpeech.transcribeFile` on them while the engine is the device, with Hermes as the fallback. Read aloud (`voice/read_aloud.dart`, `chat/widgets/read_aloud_actions.dart`) is a finished reply's action after Copy, offered only while `voice-config` says the chat's profile can speak (`ChatScreen` reads it per profile and provides a `ReadAloud`, needing a `MediaStore`; conversation windows and the quick panel offer none): Play sends the reply's words without their Markdown marks to `POST /api/audio/speak`, stages the audio in `MediaStore` and plays it on the transcript's one `VoicePlayback`, the voice messages' player, so nothing plays twice and a voice call disables it; Stop starts it over next time. Breadcrumb `chat.read_aloud` (`action`: start, stop, failed). The hands-free voice conversation is planned in `openspec/changes/voice-conversation/`. Voice calls (iOS, `lib/src/voice/calls/`, `packages/hermes_calls`, `docs/voice-calls.md`) log `voice_call.readiness` (each check's status and the `callErrorKind` of one that threw), `voice_call.connected` once CallKit hands over the audio and one `voice_call.ended` (`outcome`, `reason`, `step`, `error_type`, `connected`, bucketed `turns`, `speech_fallbacks` and `duration`); each `/api/audio/speak-stream` socket gets its own gateway tracer. Fixed words only, never speech, text, URLs or ids.

**Call widgets (catalog only).** The design for the hands-free conversation (`docs/design-briefs/voice-conversation.md`) starts as plain-model widgets in `lib/src/voice/widgets/` with use cases in `widgetbook/voice_call_use_cases.dart`; none is wired to a controller yet except the two views the shipped call screen already builds. `VoiceOrb` is the 112 pt indicator with one icon per `VoiceOrbState` (connecting, listening, muted, sending, thinking, speaking, waiting, stopped), two level rings that follow `amplitude` and stand still under `reduceMotion` or the system's disable-animations flag, and a tap (the interrupt) only while speaking. `HandsFreeStrip` is the pill for the app shell (dot, "Hands-free on", the microphone's state, an optional 44 pt End; at 1.6x text or more it drops the second half); it is not placed anywhere yet. `VoiceCallNotice` and `VoiceConsentRow` are the notice card and the consent card. `VoiceCallView` gained optional parameters (`sending`, `amplitude`, `showSpeaker`, `speakingHint`, `onInterrupt`, `notice`, `endedByUser`, `stoppedWord`, `capturePaused`, `onStartNew`, `footer`; there is no `CallPhase.sending` yet, so `transcribing` reads as thinking), and the shipped screen's look and words change with it, which is everything a user of it sees differently: (1) the orb (112 pt, one icon per state) replaces the old tile and the "Hermes" headline; (2) the state words are Connecting…, Listening, Muted, Sending (only when the caller says `sending`), Thinking, Speaking, Waiting for you, Call ended and Call interrupted (Stopped where `stoppedWord` is set); "Understanding…" is gone: transcribing reads "Thinking" until a later PR gives it a phase; (3) each state has a hint line: Listening "Speak, then pause. Hermes sends what you said.", Muted "Hermes can't hear you. Unmute to talk." (not "nothing is captured": the iOS microphone tap stays installed and the engine keeps running while muted, and `CallAudio` only drops the samples, so the sentence says what is true), Sending "Hermes is getting your words ready. End or mute now to cancel.", Thinking "Hermes is working on your request.", Speaking one of three tap and talk variants (the tap hint only with `onInterrupt`), Waiting for you "Hermes needs your input in chat." (the paused-capture text only with `capturePaused`); Connecting and the stopped states have none; (4) the footer sentence "Speak naturally. Pause to send. You can interrupt Hermes." is removed; (5) End is a filled neutral circle instead of red, Mute, Speaker and End are 56 pt circles with a label under each, and a selected toggle is ringed; (6) the controls and the route line are pinned to the bottom and the rest scrolls, so End never moves when a caption, a notice or larger text appears or changes (tested at 1x and 2x); (7) the level rings behind the orb are drawn only when an `amplitude` is passed, and the shipped screen passes none yet, so Listening and Speaking show no halo; (8) the stopped call swaps its buttons: "Back to chat" is filled, "Ask Hermes for help" (after a failure) and "Start a new call" are outlined; (9) on the setup view Start call and its neighbours sit in a pinned bar once every check passed; (10) the caption and any notice share a slot of fixed height (four lines of the current text size, at most 22% of the window) right above the controls, so what was heard and what went wrong are always in view, a longer text scrolls inside the slot, and the old fixed 120 pt caption slot is gone; (11) a window shorter than 600 pt (a phone on its side) lays out in two columns, the orb, state word, hint and timer on the left and Show chat, the caption or notice and the controls on the right, so the state word is visible at 1x; the pinned controls are capped at half the window height (65% in landscape) and scroll inside themselves at large text, so End stays reachable; (12) the timer is smaller (bodySmall), and a selected Mute or Speaker keeps only its ring and no longer gets the primaryContainer fill; (13) the setup view's ready sentence reads "Talk to Hermes without using your hands. Your microphone is closed until you start." instead of "Your microphone and speech settings are ready."; (14) the call view's and setup view's strings now come from the ARB files (`context.l10n`, German exists), but only English shows while `kReleasedLocales` lists English alone. Callbacks, control names and the old tests are unchanged. `VoiceCallSetupView` keeps its old behaviour unless `onConsentChanged` is set: then Start call is disabled until `consented`, the consent switch, Start and "Use dictation instead" stay pinned under a scrolling body (at 1.3x text the sentence and the note move into the body), and `gptLiveNote` and `footer` add the GPT-Live note and the platform line. All their strings are in the ARB files under the voice area. `CallCheck.label` lets a row carry a name the readiness checker does not produce yet (the voice model).
