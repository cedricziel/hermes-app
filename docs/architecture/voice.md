# Voice

**Voice** (`lib/src/voice/`). Dictation follows assistant-ui's dictation element: the composer's microphone (shown when `GET /api/audio/voice-config` reports usable speech-to-text for the chat's profile; `stt disabled` or `no credentials` hide it) records 16 kHz mono PCM with `record` (`voice_recorder.dart`), and, after the Claude app, the field stays read-only with the words recognized so far at the cursor (`DictationDraft`, which gives the draft back on cancel or failure) while the controls row shows cancel, the waveform (`widgets/voice_waveform.dart`) with the engine's name, stop and send. Send while dictating stops, waits for the transcript (`DictationController.whenSettled`) and sends the draft, but nothing after a failed transcription; otherwise the transcript is never sent. `DictationController` streams the audio to the `/api/audio/transcribe-stream` socket when `stt.streaming` says the provider can transcribe live (`TranscribeStream`, opened by `hermesMixedSocketConnect` with the same ticket or token as `/api/ws`), and otherwise, or when that fails, uploads the recording as WAV through `HermesChatRepository.transcribe` with a longer timeout (`receiveTimeoutExtra`, applied by `RequestTimeoutInterceptor` on every client `AuthController` builds). A connector that throws (even synchronously) only fails the live stream, so the upload takes over; any other failure while starting ends the dictation as failed, once, and nothing is sent. Stopping the recorder is attempted on every path (a recorder that throws on stop is contained, even synchronously, and the microphone is not guaranteed closed then): a recorder whose `start` throws, or whose stream a superseded start cannot listen to, is stopped; a recorder that throws on stop ends `stop()` as failed (once, nothing sent) and `cancel()` as cancelled, never leaving the phase stuck or throwing. It holds an `stt-lease` while recording, drops the recording when the app leaves the foreground or the profile changes, and records breadcrumbs without text. On iOS and macOS dictation defaults to the device's own recognizer (`DictationSettings`; the Dictation setting in the account menu and Settings… switches to Hermes, and a language the device cannot recognize falls back to it): `packages/hermes_speech` (a local plugin, one Swift source for both) runs Apple's `SpeechAnalyzer` on the same PCM, and `OnDeviceSpeech` wraps its channels, one app-wide instance because the event channel has a single native listener. With it no `/api/audio/` route is called and no recording is kept; `ChatScreen` fetches a missing model by itself and shows the microphone once it is installed (each app has to request it once even when the system already has it); the waveform says which engine is listening. Conversation windows offer no dictation, so neither `record` nor `hermes_speech` is registered in their engine. The watch has no recognizer (watchOS has no Speech framework); its voice messages reach the phone as AAC (`transcribe`), and `WatchBridge.onDeviceTranscriber` runs `OnDeviceSpeech.transcribeFile` on them while the engine is the device, with Hermes as the fallback. Read aloud (`voice/read_aloud.dart`, `chat/widgets/read_aloud_actions.dart`) is a finished reply's action after Copy, offered only while `voice-config` says the chat's profile can speak (`ChatScreen` reads it per profile and provides a `ReadAloud`, needing a `MediaStore`; conversation windows and the quick panel offer none): Play sends the reply's words without their Markdown marks to `POST /api/audio/speak`, stages the audio in `MediaStore` and plays it on the transcript's one `VoicePlayback`, the voice messages' player, so nothing plays twice and a voice call disables it; Stop starts it over next time. Breadcrumb `chat.read_aloud` (`action`: start, stop, failed). The hands-free voice conversation (`openspec/changes/voice-conversation/`) ships in parts; **Hands-free calls** below says what exists and what is still planned. Voice calls (iOS, `lib/src/voice/calls/`, `packages/hermes_calls`, `docs/voice-calls.md`) log `voice_call.readiness` (each check's status and the `callErrorKind` of one that threw), `voice_call.connected` once CallKit hands over the audio and one `voice_call.ended` (`outcome`, `reason`, `step`, `error_type`, `connected`, bucketed `turns`, `speech_fallbacks`, `speech_retries` and `duration`) plus one `voice_call.turn` per sent reply with latency buckets (`callLatencyBucket`) and its outcome; a failed `speech-stream` open is retried once after 500 ms when `isTransientSpeechOpenError` says it may pass, before the one-piece fallback; each `/api/audio/speak-stream` socket gets its own gateway tracer. Fixed words only, never speech, text, URLs or ids.

**Hands-free calls** (`lib/src/voice/calls/`, `lib/src/voice/hands_free_strip_layer.dart`; `docs/voice-calls.md` has the user-facing rules). `VoiceCall` owns the loop; `CallBridge` is its audio seam (`events`: `activated`, `pcm`, `muted`, `route`, `audioReset`, `deactivated`, `ended`, `error`, `capturePaused`; `start`, `connected`, `mute`, `speaker`, `play`, `drain`, `stopPlayback`, `end`, and since this change `pauseCapture`, `resumeCapture` and `stopCapture`). `AppleCallBridge` (iOS, CallKit) and `PortableCallBridge` (Android, macOS, Windows, Linux) implement it; `createCallBridge` (`portable_call_audio.dart`) picks one. **Consent:** `VoiceCall` takes `consented` and never opens capture without it; the setup view's switch is local state, off on every open, never stored (a transcript is never sent without it). **Phases:** `CallPhase.sending` lasts until the transport is about to write `prompt.submit` (`onSubmitted`, which is `ChatTransport.send`'s `onSubmitting`); the call's `wanted` predicate (epoch, requested mute, no approval waiting, consent) goes to `CallAgent.reply` and from there to `ChatController.submitVoiceTurn` and the transport's `authorize`, so End or mute before the write sends nothing, including during the queue restore and `session.resume`. **Turn outcomes:** `ChatCallAgent` throws `CallTurnRefused` (the typed `VoiceTurnRefusal`) and reports `CallTurnAnswer` (`queued`, `steered`, `redirected`, `voiceStopped`); `VoiceCall` maps them to `CallNotice`s (busy, queuePaused, notSent, hermesQueued, hermesSteered, hermesRedirected), ends on a lost session or profile (`error_type` `session_reclaimed` or `profile_not_confirmed`) and on `voice_stopped`. **`interrupted`:** `VoiceCall` arms it when the user cuts off speech (orb tap, or onset while speaking), asks for it on the next turn (`agent.reply(interrupted:)`), keeps it across refused, dropped and echoed turns, and clears it on the first turn that reaches the transport, on mute, a route change, a typed message (`CallAgent.watchTyped`) and after 120 s (`_interruptedTtl`); the controller sends it only for a settled thread. **Filters:** one order, empty, echo (`VoiceCall.echoGuard`, by default the call's own `EchoGuard`, fed through `replySpoken`), `isWhisperHallucination` (a port of Hermes' filter at the pinned revision), stop phrase, else sent. **Config:** `VoiceCallConfig` reads the profile's `voice` keys with `include_defaults=false` (`silence_duration` clamped 0.5 to 3 s, `stop_phrases` with the built-in list when absent, `barge_in`). **Teardown:** `_end` runs once: `stopCapture` first, then the speech socket and playback, the stop tone, the audio session and focus, the `tts-lease`, last the agent, each step with its own 2 s timeout and guard; an unconfirmed `stopCapture` sets `microphoneUnconfirmed` and the notice "may still be open". **Waiting for you** is tracked per call and read from the thread, pauses capture and resets the detector; the stall watchdog is suspended while capture is paused by design; the idle limit is 5 minutes. Barge-in stops playback and the socket only, and the reply is interrupted when a real turn is about to be sent. `audioReset` drops the turn but keeps the reply and Waiting for you. Tones and haptics are behind seams (`CallTones`, `CallHaptics`) and play with capture paused. **Readiness:** `CallReadinessChecker` has one `CallCheck` per `CallCheckId` (microphone, input, output, `voiceModel`, connection); `CallCheckStatus.warning` (a voice model that does not resolve or whose read failed) does not block `ready`; `VoiceModelRoute` parses the `voice_chat` row of `GET /api/model/auxiliary` and `voice-live/status` gives the GPT-Live note. **Strip:** `ActiveCallRegistry` (provided in `voiceProviders`) is told by `ChatScreen` about the call and tells the lock cover and `HandsFreeStripLayer` what the strip says (state only) and offers End. The layer wraps the app's navigator in `app.dart`'s builder, so it sits under the platform bar (the top inset) on every page of the main window, including pages pushed over the shell, and carries End while the call screen is closed (`screenOpened`/`screenClosed`, owner-checked); conversation windows and the quick panel never build it. A call whose chat is disposed (sign-out, owner generation) is handed to `ActiveCallRegistry.release`, which ends it first and keeps the "may still be open" state, because a disposed call tells nobody. **Portable bridge** (`portable_call_bridge.dart`, `portable_call_audio.dart`). `PortableCallBridge` takes three seams (`CallRecorder`, `CallPlayer`, `CallAudioSession`) and has no platform code of its own; `RecordCallRecorder` (`record`, 16 kHz mono, echo cancel and noise suppress on, the Android voice-communication source, no audio focus of its own, route changes followed), `SoLoudCallPlayer` (`flutter_soloud` 5.3.0: one released buffer stream per run of audio at one rate, 0.15 s lead-in, `drain` waits for the stream's end event, `lowLatency: false` and the voice-communication AAudio usage on Android) and `AndroidCallAudioSession` (`audio_session`: voice-communication focus request, in-communication mode, speakerphone) are the real ones. Nothing is created or started before `start`, which the call makes after consent. A closed microphone is a stopped recorder: `pauseCapture`, `mute`, `stopCapture` and the playback block all stop it and `resumeCapture` restarts it, so the OS indicator goes out. `start` is cancellable (a generation counter): an End or `stopCapture` while `record.startStream` is pending closes the capture when it resolves and emits no `activated`. Without barge-in (the constructor flag, false everywhere today) it also blocks the microphone while audio plays and for 400 ms after (`echoTail`), computed from the bytes played (a tone has no `drain`), and says so with `capturePaused` reasons `request` and `playback`. `VoiceCall` pauses capture in every phase but Listening where `bargeInActive` is false, so the microphone is closed through Sending, Thinking and Speaking, not only while audio plays. Focus loss other than ducking is `deactivated` (`reason: focus`); a device change is `audioReset` (capture reopened; ignored for 800 ms after the app's own speaker switch); a recorder stream that fails or ends is `error` (`audio_start`); a microphone that cannot start fails `start`. `SoLoud` is never initialised on iOS and its plugin is not registered in the macOS conversation-window or quick-panel engines. `EchoGuard` keeps the reply the call is speaking and spends itself on the first transcript after it (similarity 0.6 on lowercased words; a window of the reply for transcripts of ten characters or more, up to 200 characters, against the last 4000 characters of the reply). The screen shows Speaker on iOS and Android only, the platform footer, and the speaking hint that fits (`tapOrTalk` with barge-in, `tapOnly` on Android, `micPaused` on desktop); `VoiceCall.route` is empty off iOS and the view then draws no route line. The call opens through `showVoiceCall` (`call_presentation.dart`): a full-screen page below 760 pt, a 480 pt dialog from 760 pt and on every Mac width. Still planned: the turn list and the "Speak replies" switch.

**Call widgets.** The design for the hands-free conversation (`docs/design-briefs/voice-conversation.md`) lives as plain-model widgets in `lib/src/voice/widgets/` with use cases in `widgetbook/voice_call_use_cases.dart`. `VoiceOrb` is the 112 pt indicator with one icon per `VoiceOrbState` (connecting, listening, muted, sending, thinking, speaking, waiting, stopped), two level rings that follow `amplitude` and stand still under `reduceMotion` or the system's disable-animations flag, and a tap (the interrupt) only while speaking. `HandsFreeStrip` is the pill (dot, "Hands-free on", the microphone's state, an optional 44 pt End; at 1.6x text or more it drops the second half; `HandsFreeMicrophone.mayBeOpen` is one whole phrase with a warning glyph). `VoiceCallNotice` and `VoiceConsentRow` are the notice card and the consent card. Content that scrolls in the call view (the state area, the notice and caption slot, the controls box) fades at the edge that has more behind it. `VoiceCallView` gained optional parameters (`sending`, `amplitude`, `showSpeaker`, `speakingHint`, `onInterrupt`, `notice`, `endedByUser`, `stoppedWord`, `capturePaused`, `onStartNew`, `footer`; `CallPhase.sending` is Sending), and the shipped screen's look and words change with it, which is everything a user of it sees differently: (1) the orb (112 pt, one icon per state) replaces the old tile and the "Hermes" headline; (2) the state words are Connecting…, Listening, Muted, Sending (only when the caller says `sending`), Thinking, Speaking, Waiting for you, Call ended and Call interrupted (Stopped where `stoppedWord` is set); "Understanding…" is gone; (3) each state has a hint line: Listening "Speak, then pause. Hermes sends what you said.", Muted "Hermes can't hear you. Unmute to talk." (not "nothing is captured": the iOS microphone tap stays installed and the engine keeps running while muted, and `CallAudio` only drops the samples, so the sentence says what is true), Sending "Hermes is getting your words ready. End or mute now to cancel.", Thinking "Hermes is working on your request.", Speaking one of three tap and talk variants (the tap hint only with `onInterrupt`), Waiting for you "Hermes needs your input in chat." (the paused-capture text only with `capturePaused`); Connecting and the stopped states have none; (4) the footer sentence "Speak naturally. Pause to send. You can interrupt Hermes." is removed; (5) End is a filled neutral circle instead of red, Mute, Speaker and End are 56 pt circles with a label under each, and a selected toggle is ringed; (6) the controls and the route line are pinned to the bottom and the rest scrolls, so End never moves when a caption, a notice or larger text appears or changes (tested at 1x and 2x); (7) the level rings behind the orb are drawn only when an `amplitude` is passed, and the shipped screen passes none yet, so Listening and Speaking show no halo; (8) the stopped call swaps its buttons: "Back to chat" is filled, "Ask Hermes for help" (after a failure) and "Start a new call" are outlined; (9) on the setup view Start call and its neighbours sit in a pinned bar once every check passed; (10) the notice and the caption share a slot right above the controls (at least four lines of the current text size and 18% of the window, at most 30%): the notice comes first, the caption under it shows its last lines when it is long, and a text longer than its part scrolls inside the slot, so short content is in view and nothing pushes the controls; the old fixed 120 pt caption slot is gone; (11) the controls and route line are pinned to the bottom (capped at 45% of the window, scrolling inside themselves), so End does not move with a caption, a notice or the text size; the three controls sit in fixed-width cells on one row, their visible labels stop growing at 1.15x (screen readers use the button name), and Show chat stops at 1.3x; at large text the orb is dropped when the state area is too short, so the state word is always the first thing in view; (12) a window shorter than 600 pt (a phone on its side) lays out in two columns (at most 880 pt wide), the orb, state word, hint and timer on the left and Show chat, the notice or caption and the controls on the right (capped at 65%); the state word shrinks to fit rather than breaking mid-word; (13) the timer is smaller (bodySmall), a selected Mute or Speaker keeps only its ring (no primaryContainer fill), and the setup view's ready sentence reads "Talk to Hermes without using your hands. Your microphone is closed until you start." instead of "Your microphone and speech settings are ready."; (14) the call view's and setup view's strings now come from the ARB files (`context.l10n`, German exists), but only English shows while `kReleasedLocales` lists English alone. Callbacks, control names and the old tests are unchanged. `VoiceCallSetupView` keeps its old behaviour unless `onConsentChanged` is set: then Start call is disabled until `consented`, the consent switch, Start and "Use dictation instead" stay pinned under a scrolling body (at 1.3x text the sentence and the note move into the body), and `gptLiveNote` and `footer` add the GPT-Live note and the platform line. All their strings are in the ARB files under the voice area.
