# Voice

**Voice** (`lib/src/voice/`). Dictation follows assistant-ui's dictation element: the composer's microphone (shown when `GET /api/audio/voice-config` reports usable speech-to-text for the chat's profile; `stt disabled` or `no credentials` hide it) records 16 kHz mono PCM with `record` (`voice_recorder.dart`), and, after the Claude app, the field stays read-only with the words recognized so far at the cursor (`DictationDraft`, which gives the draft back on cancel or failure) while the controls row shows cancel, the waveform (`widgets/voice_waveform.dart`) with the engine's name, stop and send. Send while dictating stops, waits for the transcript (`DictationController.whenSettled`) and sends the draft, but nothing after a failed transcription; otherwise the transcript is never sent. `DictationController` streams the audio to the `/api/audio/transcribe-stream` socket when `stt.streaming` says the provider can transcribe live (`TranscribeStream`, opened by `hermesMixedSocketConnect` with the same ticket or token as `/api/ws`), and otherwise, or when that fails, uploads the recording as WAV through `HermesChatRepository.transcribe` with a longer timeout (`receiveTimeoutExtra`, applied by `RequestTimeoutInterceptor` on every client `AuthController` builds). A connector that throws (even synchronously) only fails the live stream, so the upload takes over; any other failure while starting ends the dictation as failed, once, and nothing is sent. Stopping the recorder is attempted on every path (a recorder that throws on stop is contained, even synchronously, and the microphone is not guaranteed closed then): a recorder whose `start` throws, or whose stream a superseded start cannot listen to, is stopped; a recorder that throws on stop ends `stop()` as failed (once, nothing sent) and `cancel()` as cancelled, never leaving the phase stuck or throwing. It holds an `stt-lease` while recording, drops the recording when the app leaves the foreground or the profile changes, and records breadcrumbs without text. On iOS and macOS dictation defaults to the device's own recognizer (`DictationSettings`; the Dictation setting in the account menu and Settings… switches to Hermes, and a language the device cannot recognize falls back to it): `packages/hermes_speech` (a local plugin, one Swift source for both) runs Apple's `SpeechAnalyzer` on the same PCM, and `OnDeviceSpeech` wraps its channels, one app-wide instance because the event channel has a single native listener. With it no `/api/audio/` route is called and no recording is kept; `ChatScreen` fetches a missing model by itself and shows the microphone once it is installed (each app has to request it once even when the system already has it); the waveform says which engine is listening. Conversation windows offer no dictation, so neither `record` nor `hermes_speech` is registered in their engine. The watch has no recognizer (watchOS has no Speech framework); its voice messages reach the phone as AAC (`transcribe`), and `WatchBridge.onDeviceTranscriber` runs `OnDeviceSpeech.transcribeFile` on them while the engine is the device, with Hermes as the fallback. Read aloud (`voice/read_aloud.dart`, `chat/widgets/read_aloud_actions.dart`) is a finished reply's action after Copy, offered only while `voice-config` says the chat's profile can speak (`ChatScreen` reads it per profile and provides a `ReadAloud`, needing a `MediaStore`; conversation windows and the quick panel offer none): Play sends the reply's words without their Markdown marks to `POST /api/audio/speak`, stages the audio in `MediaStore` and plays it on the transcript's one `VoicePlayback`, the voice messages' player, so nothing plays twice and a voice call disables it; Stop starts it over next time. Breadcrumb `chat.read_aloud` (`action`: start, stop, failed). The hands-free voice conversation is planned in `openspec/changes/voice-conversation/`. Voice calls (iOS, `lib/src/voice/calls/`, `packages/hermes_calls`, `docs/voice-calls.md`) log `voice_call.readiness` (each check's status and the `callErrorKind` of one that threw), `voice_call.connected` once CallKit hands over the audio and one `voice_call.ended` (`outcome`, `reason`, `step`, `error_type`, `connected`, bucketed `turns`, `speech_fallbacks` and `duration`); each `/api/audio/speak-stream` socket gets its own gateway tracer. Fixed words only, never speech, text, URLs or ids.
